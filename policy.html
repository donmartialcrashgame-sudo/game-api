1. Privacy

Gaming API may process information necessary to provide accounts, authentication, API access, security, support, and service operation. We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Information may include account details, authentication information, technical information, logs, and support communications. Payment information, where applicable, may be handled by the relevant payment provider.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, analytics, and service functionality. Third-party support tools such as Smartsupp may process information when the chat service is loaded or used.

3. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect accounts and service data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your passwords, tokens, and devices.

4. Acceptable use

  • Do not abuse, disrupt, overload, or interfere with the service.
  • Do not attempt unauthorized access or bypass authentication.
  • Do not use the API to distribute malware, conduct fraud, or violate applicable law.
  • Do not scrape, reverse engineer, or automate access in a way that violates published limits or security controls.
  • Do not share private credentials or another user's account information.

5. Service availability

Services may change, be temporarily unavailable, or be discontinued. We may introduce limits, maintenance windows, security controls, or feature changes when reasonably necessary.

6. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable law.

7. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the service evolves. Material changes may be reflected by updating the date shown on this page.

8. Contact

For policy or privacy questions, use the website's support chat or the support contact provided with your account.
